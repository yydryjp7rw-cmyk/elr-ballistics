Truing to a real hit

Set the weather and rifle above to match the day of a confirmed hit, enter that hit, then press True it. One hit can be matched two ways. They agree near the hit distance and spread apart farther away.

Dope table

Barrel angle is how far the bore points above level, the reading an inclinometer gives on a surface parallel to the bore, such as the receiver. A tapered scope rail reads lower by the rail's built-in MOA (20 MOA = 0.33°, 40 MOA = 0.67°).

Point-mass solver with standard G1/G7 drag, air that thins as the bullet climbs, Litz spin drift and full Coriolis. Elevation is solved as the barrel-angle change from your zero. With the .375 preset it matches a separately written solver within 0.25 MOA out to 3.25 miles and 1.6 MOA at 5 miles. Real holds at extreme range have run higher than any solver, so true it to your own hits.
